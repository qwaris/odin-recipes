Two-Ingredient Pizza Dough

Description

This 2-ingredient pizza dough is fast and easy to make and tastes great! Greek yogurt is the secret ingredient and baking the crust at a higher temperature is the key to making this the perfect pizza. It doesn't get any easier than this folks. Brush dough with olive oil, add your favorite toppings, and enjoy!

  • 1 ½ cups self-rising flour, plus more for kneading
  • 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • cooking spray

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients. Preheat the oven to 500 degrees F (260 degrees C).
  2. Mix flour and Greek yogurt together in a bowl to form a dough; transfer to a work surface floured with self-rising flour.
  3. Knead dough, adding more flour as needed to keep dough from being too sticky, about 8 to 10 minutes.
  4. Spray a 12-inch pizza pan with cooking spray and spread dough to the edges of the pan.
  5. Bake pizza crust in preheated oven for 5 to 7 minutes; add your favorite toppings and return to the preheated oven.
  6. Bake until crust is lightly browned, 5 to 7 minutes more.
  7. Enjoy!
